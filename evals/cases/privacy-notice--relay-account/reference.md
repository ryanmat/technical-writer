# Privacy notice for your Relay account

This notice tells you what Northwind Labs does with the personal data in your Relay account, why, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

## At a glance

Northwind Labs runs Relay and is responsible for your data. We use your name, work email and company to run your account and bill your company; we use route and log data to keep Relay secure; and we send you product emails only if you have agreed to them. You have the right to see, correct, delete and move your data and to object to how we use it. We keep logs for 13 months. We share data with the providers that host Relay, send our emails and process payments, and we do not sell it. The sections below give the full notice in one place.

## Who we are

Northwind Labs is responsible for your data. Write to our data protection officer at privacy@relay.example.com or to Northwind Labs, 12 Harbour Street, Dublin 2, Ireland.

## What we use your data for

- To run your account and deliver Relay: your name, work email and company. Legal basis: our contract with your company.
- To keep Relay secure and to find faults: the routes you create and the access logs that record your sign-ins and API calls. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure service.
- To answer your support requests: the messages you send to support. Legal basis: our contract with your company.
- To send you product emails: your name and work email. Legal basis: your consent, which you give in your account settings and can withdraw there at any time.
- To keep accounting records: invoices that name your company and the account holder. Legal basis: our legal obligation to keep accounting records.

## Who we share your data with

- The cloud provider that hosts Relay in the European Union.
- The email provider that sends our account and product emails.
- The payment processor that bills your company.
- Authorities, when the law requires us to.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

## Where your data goes

Relay runs in the European Union. Our email provider processes your name and work email in the United States under standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## How long we keep your data

- Account data: for as long as your account exists, then 90 days.
- Routes and access logs: 13 months.
- Invoices: 7 years, as accounting law requires.
- Support messages: [UNVERIFIED: the retention period for support messages, which legal has not yet set]

## Your rights

You have the right to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, to receive it in a file you can take elsewhere, and to object to our use of it where we rely on our legitimate interest. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time in your account settings. To use any of these rights, write to privacy@relay.example.com; we answer within one month.

## How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, write to our data protection officer first. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live. Our lead authority is the Data Protection Commission in Ireland.

## Where your data comes from

Most of your data comes from you when you use Relay. Your name, work email and company come from the administrator at your company who created your account.
